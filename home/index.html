A FEW IMPORTANT ANSWERS

Clear by design.

No fine-print promises about something this important.

Does the person helping me need an app?

The Assist web card is designed to open from a compatible phone’s NFC reader or camera without a RedMed account. Device compatibility and a usable web connection or previously cached shell are required.

Can I activate my NFC band here?

This portal can register a chip’s serial number on supported Web NFC devices, or let you enter it manually. That is an inventory record, not medical-card activation. The native iOS writing and read-back flow remains a separate, gated feature.

What happens without internet?

A previously loaded Assist shell may work from its cache. A phone that has never opened it cannot rely on offline access. Keep essential engraved information or another non-digital backup.

Does marking a band lost disable it?

No. Statuses here are private inventory labels. A local payload on a band cannot be remotely revoked or rewritten through this account.

Is RedMed ready for medical use?

RedMed is in development. Clinical-content review, physical-band validation and production-origin readiness are still release gates. It is not a replacement for emergency services or professional medical care.

DATA BOUNDARIES / SEPTEMBER 2026

What your account holds.

Account data

Supabase Auth processes your email and password for authentication. The band registry stores your account ID, chip serial, color, inventory status and registration date. Database policies restrict these records to your authenticated account.

What stays out

Do not enter diagnoses, emergency contacts, care instructions, medical-card URLs or band payloads here. This portal does not collect them. NFC reading uses only the tag serial number and never reads or uploads NDEF record content.

Important limits

A chip UID is not proof of ownership and is not an authentication secret. Changing a band’s inventory status does not revoke its payload. Anyone with that payload can read its wearer-selected information.

Session and infrastructure

Sign-in stays in this browser until you sign out. Supabase processes account requests; hosting infrastructure may process request metadata such as IP addresses. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs on this portal.

Administrator access

Authorized administrators can view account emails, verification status, band counts and account dates, invite members, and suspend or restore account access. Administrative actions are audited. The admin panel does not expose medical information or band payloads.

Your controls

You can remove band records, change your password, and request an email recovery link. Email delivery depends on provider configuration. Full account deletion is not yet self-service; do not use this development portal for a medical-care dependency.
