A FEW IMPORTANT ANSWERS

Clear by design.

No fine-print promises about something this important.

When does it ship?

Ships when ready. There is no date promise. Your Square receipt is your order record.

What am I buying today?

Blank NTAG216 bands. Writing a band from inside the app is not live yet, so this page does not sell it.

Is it a medical device or HIPAA certified?

No. It is an ID card on a band, not a medical device and not HIPAA certified.

Is my payment safe?

Checkout is hosted by Square. This page loads no payment scripts and stores no card data.
